Happy Chamber Day, Delaware Area Chamber

Here's a simple plan for Support Your Local Chamber Day and the week after: one message, six posts, and a small boost. The goal is to tell former and future members there's new leadership, real momentum and a lot going on.

01

The strategy

Message

One message.

Serving Delaware County since 1907, and we've never had more going on. Every post proves it with one fact: 13 new members, a full education calendar, nearly 1,000 Leadership Delaware graduates, and a new young professionals group.

Audience

Former members first.

About 1,000 businesses used to be members. Susie's email reaches 100 of them personally, and the posts back it up in public. The rejoin offer (a free newsletter ad, rejoin by Nov 20) gives them a reason to act now.

Channels

Three channels, one set of posts.

Facebook is home base. The same posts go to LinkedIn (and Susie's own profile, which reaches further than the page) and to the Google Business Profile as Updates, which helps local search at no cost.

About boosting Boost only the Chamber Day post. $50 to $100 over 3 to 5 days, people in Delaware County ages 25 to 65, goal set to website visits or messages, not likes. Hold the rest of the budget for a second test in November. Ad spend is paid to Facebook directly.

04

Your Chamber Day email

Your email is warm and ready. Five small changes will help it land:

  1. Greet people by first name instead of "Dear Former Chamber Member."
  2. Add a "Rejoin here" button that links to the membership form, plus Melissa's direct phone and email.
  3. Use the number: "13 new members joined last month."
  4. Pick one Young Professionals number (the email says 65, the flyer says more than 60).
  5. Mention that you're an accredited chamber. No other chamber nearby is.
Tip Send it as a regular email with the flyer attached, 20 to 25 a day from your own address. An email that's one big image is more likely to land in spam.

Subject idea: "A lot has changed at the Chamber (and a welcome-back gift)."
