An idea: make it Chamber Week

Other chambers turned Chamber Day into a whole week, with a promo each day. Here's how yours could look. Post it first thing Wednesday morning.

Want to add a promo?

The example on the left follows another chamber's Chamber Week: free coffee, a proclamation from the mayor, and lunch with the Chamber. Anything in [brackets] is a placeholder. Tell us which ones you can line up (a member café for coffee, the mayor's office, a lunch spot) and we'll finish it in minutes. More ideas:

  • Free coffee at a member coffee shop or cafe for the first 25 people who stop by
  • No application fee for anyone who joins during Chamber Week
  • Bring a guest free to Business Before Hours (Nov 5) or After Hours (Nov 19)
  • A member deals day: members share a special for other members, and you share them

Just tell us who and what, and we'll update it.
